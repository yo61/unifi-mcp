# Knowledge index

Facts, open hypotheses and confirmed rules, by domain. Rules apply by default; a
hypothesis confirmed three or more times is promoted to a rule, and a rule that
new data contradicts is demoted back. Decisions with lasting consequences live
in `/decisions/`, and the checks that gate completion in `/quality/criteria.md`.
